# Sovereign AI Club

Wednesday, 5:30 to 7:30 PM Central (America/Chicago). Free admission.

Bitcoin Park Austin, 601 Congress Avenue, Suite 250, Austin, TX 78701.

- **Same model, different harness: 8090's DeepSWE run** - 8090 Labs ran seven models through five harnesses on 113 real DeepSWE tasks, and no model scored best in its maker's own harness. Claude Fable 5.1 passed 41% in [[Claude Code]] and 61% in [[Codex]], the only gap that clears 8090's noise bound, and [[GLM]] 5.3 Flash swung from 24% in Codex to 59% in the DeepSeek harness.
  Source: https://www.8090.ai/blog/same-model-different-harness
  Post: https://x.com/chamath/status/2109703737493344363

- **Hugging Face trains open models inside Claude Code and Codex** - [[Hugging Face]] built a proxy that turns unmodified harnesses, including [[Claude Code]] and [[Codex]], into RL environments by recording the tokens the harness thinks came from a model API. On data-analysis tasks from Kaggle notebooks, Liquid's 2.6B LFM2.5 solved 62% under Mini-SWE-Agent and 33% under Claude Code, and training across four harnesses lifted it from 42% to 54%.

Prime's swarm is an MIT harness on a model with public weights; GPT-Rosalind is closed, and a subscription doesn't get you in.

- **dax's OpenTunnel keeps TLS on your machine** - [[OpenTunnel]] gives a local process a public hostname and terminates TLS on your machine, so the relay isn't supposed to see your HTTP traffic. Its own docs say inbound TCP still runs on temporary AWS relays, and hostnames land in public certificate logs.
  Repo: https://github.com/anomalyco/opentunnel
  Source: https://opentunnel.xyz/

- **Reason Network lets your agents message your teammates' agents** - [[Reason Network]] puts [[Claude Code]], [[Codex]], [[Pi]], and other coding agents on one peer-to-peer network, so your session can ask a teammate's agent a question mid-turn. The engine is MIT or Apache 2.0, but the join page tells the agent to pipe Reason's closed installer into bash "without asking questions," and a teammate's message can wake your session by default.
