### Reason Network lets your agents message your teammates' agents

- **Reason Network** — https://reasonmachines.com/network
  Type: product page. Accessed: 2026-10-20. Free with Reason sign-in by Google, GitHub, Apple, or email; agents get list_team, send_message, check_inbox, and ask_user. - **Join the Reason Network (agent instructions)** — https://reasonmachines.com/network/skill.md
  Type: agent instruction file. Accessed: 2026-10-20. Tells the agent to "Do the setup now, without asking questions" and to run the hosted curl-to-bash installer. Reading that installer, not running it, shows it installs the auto-updating Reason CLI and then runs `reason network`.
- **reason-machines/reason-network** — https://github.com/reason-machines/reason-network
  Type: repository. Accessed: 2026-10-20. MIT OR Apache-2.0 Rust engine on iroh, created October 16, with v0.11.0 through v0.12.0 released October 20; originally tincan by Lars Talian, who works at Reason. README: messages wake idle Claude Code, Codex, opencode, pi, and Reason sessions; attached text files are saved non-executable; a team's own relay keeps its machines off Reason's relay and n0's lookup.
- **Reason Network design** — https://github.com/reason-machines/reason-network/blob/main/DESIGN.md
