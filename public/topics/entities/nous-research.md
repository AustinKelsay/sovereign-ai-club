# Nous Research

Nous Research is an open AI research collective known for the Hermes model family and agent tooling experiments. In November 2026 it released Arete-27B, an Apache 2.0 fine-tune of Qwen3.8-27B that Nous says cuts answers repeating China's official line from 22% to 1% on its own eval.
