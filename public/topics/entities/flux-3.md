# FLUX 3

FLUX 3 is Black Forest Labs' multimodal flow model that jointly learns image, video, and audio in one architecture and extends that backbone to action prediction. On October 15, 2026 the lab released FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B open-weight image model under the FLUX Kommunity License, which allows free commercial use under $5 million in annual revenue.
