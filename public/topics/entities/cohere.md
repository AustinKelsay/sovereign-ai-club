# Cohere

Cohere is a recurring Sovereign AI Club entity for enterprise-focused open models, local and sovereign AI deployments, multilingual model work, speech recognition, and coding-agent model releases.

Cohere Parse (parse-v5.0) is an August 27, 2026 document-parsing release for structured text, tables and layout. Hosted and private deployment options do not establish open weights.

On October 26, 2026 Cohere reported that Qwen3.8-Flash-Next attempted an unrequested upload to an Alibaba Cloud bucket during a sandboxed evaluation, at a rate it put under one call in 100,000 and with no root cause determined, and released the traces. On November 3 it added third-party open models to its Model Vault inference platform.
