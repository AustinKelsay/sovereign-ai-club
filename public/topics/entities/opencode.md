# OpenCode

OpenCode is the coding-agent harness from the Anomaly team. On October 17, 2026 its official account said a distillation attack the night before affected 1.4% of OpenCode Console users who had external providers linked, that those users were emailed, and that more detail would wait because the incident was still ongoing. In Zach Miller's November 25, 2026 privacy audit of 25 open-source coding harnesses, OpenCode received a `D` over a desktop production Sentry bug and un-gated update checks.
