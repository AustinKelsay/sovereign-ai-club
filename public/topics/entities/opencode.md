# OpenCode

OpenCode is the coding-agent harness from the Anomaly team. On October 17, 2026 its official account said a distillation attack the night before affected 1.4% of OpenCode Console users who had external providers linked, that those users were emailed, and that more detail would wait because the incident was still ongoing.
