# Gradium

Gradium sells real-time voice APIs for agents: text-to-speech, speech recognition, translation, and voice cloning and design. On October 20, 2026 it released Phonon, its on-device text-to-speech family, as open weights. Phonon 31m speaks English under Apache 2.0; Phonon 40m, per language or one multilingual model across English, French, German, Spanish, and Portuguese, is CC BY-NC. The models build on Kyutai's Pocket TTS and Mimi codec, run on CPUs, GPUs, browsers, and phone NPUs, and can use voices cloned or designed in Gradium's cloud once downloaded. Gradium's Phonon is not Fermion Research's [[Phonon-2]] speech recognizer.
