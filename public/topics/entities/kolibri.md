# Kolibri

Kolibri-1 is Aleph Alpha's German-and-English mixture-of-experts model, released October 3, 2026. The model card lists 78B total parameters, 3.46B active, up to 1M tokens of context, and an Apache 2.0 license that covers the published weights and configuration files. Aleph Alpha says that license does not extend to the architecture, training method, or code, and serving uses their vLLM plugin. The card recommends 262K context for serving efficiency.
