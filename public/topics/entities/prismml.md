# PrismML

PrismML is a recurring Sovereign AI Club entity for extreme model compression, on-device AI, and open-source image generation. Founded by Caltech researchers with backing from Khosla Ventures, Cerberus, and Google. On November 24, 2026, PrismML released Apache 2.0 **Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B** (Qwen3.8-27B compressed to 1.76 bits/weight and 5.93 GB), reporting 75.6% SWE-bench Verified and FP16-level tool calling across GGUF and MLX builds.
