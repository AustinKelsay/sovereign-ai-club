# Strata

Strata is Niko Veit's MIT inference engine for [[Qwen|Qwen3.8-Flash-Next]] on a consumer PC. The repository was created September 24, 2026. The README says 32 GB of RAM and graphics memory together is enough to start, and that the app speaks the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs on localhost. Its own writeup says the GPU keeps the experts asked most often and RAM holds the rest. Version 0.2.2 shipped October 21, 2026, with engine packages at 0.1.47. Compressed weights are credited to ISTA-DASLab, [[Unsloth]], and others, and those files keep their own licenses.
