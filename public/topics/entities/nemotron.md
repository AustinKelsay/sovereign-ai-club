# Nemotron

Nemotron is NVIDIA's open model family, recurring in Sovereign AI Club through open-weight LLMs, speech models, reasoning/coding benchmarks, compression variants, and serving work. On October 15, 2026 NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, 120B total and 12B active, and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt, named as a 24 GB model. Both cards use the OpenMDW-1.1 license.
