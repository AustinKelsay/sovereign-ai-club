# Inception

Inception develops Mercury diffusion language models. Its September 8, 2026 Mercury 2.5 release includes hosted reasoning, with Mercury Voice and Router announced as previews requiring requested access. On October 19, 2026, Mercury 3 shipped as an API-only model with a 1M-token context. Inception's launch price is $0.10 in and $0.35 out per million tokens, half of the $0.20 and $0.70 list. Published speed figures are provider measurements.
