# Jais

Jais 3 is the Institute of Foundation Models' October 15, 2026 Arabic-native family, six Apache 2.0 models from 0.9B to a 375B mixture-of-experts flagship, built on K2 Horizon with Inception42. IFM's launch post also points at Jais Speech. Benchmark comparisons on the blog are IFM's.
