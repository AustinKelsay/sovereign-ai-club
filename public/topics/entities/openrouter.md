# OpenRouter

OpenRouter is a model routing and inference platform that gives builders a unified API for calling many AI models and routing patterns across providers.

In November 2026, after Dragos seeded two million canary prompts and caught inference resellers forwarding traffic and ignoring Zero Data Retention flags (alongside separate incidents at Prime Intellect and Together AI), OpenRouter confirmed it was not itself breached, off-boarded three providers (Atlas Cloud, Novita, and StreamLake), and stripped 125 ZDR endpoint labels.
