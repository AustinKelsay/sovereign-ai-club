# Underdog

Underdog ships on-device speech and language models. Bark 3.0, released in October 2026, is a CC-BY 4.0 English speech model. Underdog says the 163 MB download is a finetune of NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B and matches that model's 4.94% average word error. Bark 3.0 and Saluki, a separate Underdog language model, are both in the October 21 open-model feed.
