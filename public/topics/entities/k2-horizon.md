# K2 Horizon

K2 Horizon is IFM’s model family. Its Apache-2.0 MoVA 36B-A4B checkpoint routes computation through attention as well as sparse experts. As checked September 8, its card distinguishes released final weights from forthcoming training materials.

The September 3 release spans six core sizes from 0.9B through 375B-A23B, plus Uno and quantized variants. Its official collection includes datasets, while the MoVA card still promises training code, data and intermediate checkpoints; the full training release should not be treated as complete.

On October 15, 2026, IFM adapted the family into Jais 3, six Apache 2.0 Arabic-native models on the same size ladder.
