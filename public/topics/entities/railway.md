# Railway

Railway is a cloud platform for deploying apps, databases, and agents. On October 21, 2026 it disclosed that an attacker may have reached the systems storing customer environment variables, paused all deployments, and told every user to rotate the secrets they kept there. Its November 10 incident report says the attacker decrypted variables for at most 1.85% of workspaces, about 100,000, was hunting AI provider keys for distillation, and used abliterated models; unauthorized spend on stolen keys reached about $740,000.
