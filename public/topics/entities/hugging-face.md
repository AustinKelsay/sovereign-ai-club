# Hugging Face

Hugging Face hosts models, datasets, and applications and supports model discovery and distribution. NVIDIA announced an agreement to acquire the company on September 3, 2026, with stated commitments to hardware and cloud choice. On November 25, 2026, Hugging Face research published a 1,000-agent [[DeepSeek]] swarm field report (`PyBot`, 175,000 messages, 8,500+ PRs) and open-sourced `legion` and Apache 2.0 `msgboard`.
