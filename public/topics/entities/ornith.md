# Ornith

Ornith is an open-weight coding and agent model family built around self-generated training tasks, task-specific scaffolds, and solution rollouts. Its releases span edge-sized dense models and sparse frontier-scale mixtures of experts. Ornith-1.5-122B, released October 20, 2026, is an MIT-licensed mixture-of-experts model on Qwen's 3.5 MoE architecture, published in BF16 with FP8, GGUF, and MLX builds.
