# Liquid AI

Liquid AI is a recurring Sovereign AI Club entity for LFM edge/open models and structured-output evaluation work such as IFStruct. October 2026 added Open d1 on October 7 and LFM2.5-24B-A2B on October 13, both under the LFM 1.0 license. The 24B model has about 2B parameters active. On October 15, d1-ultra shipped as an API-only decision model at $0.042 per million input tokens, with no weights.
