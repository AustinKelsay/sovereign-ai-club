# Clef

Clef and Clef-flash are Cloudflare's Apache 2.0 decision models, released October 1, 2026. Cloudflare says they are Jev-API compatible, add a vision encoder and a 64K context, and score choices from frozen Qwen backbones instead of writing text. On October 9, Clef-Omni added audio and video on a Qwen3-Omni 30B-A3B backbone, also Apache 2.0. The Jev Decision Index numbers on the launch post are Cloudflare's.
